Good to know

Questions

Do all the bridesmaids need the same dress?

No. Jaylon chose the colors; the style and design are yours. Just confirm your pick with Jaylon before you order so the group looks cohesive.

Can I shop somewhere other than ChicSew or Azazie?

Those two are suggestions with lots of options in the right shades. If you find your color elsewhere, run it by Jaylon first.

When will RJ's Groom Squad attire be decided?

RJ is finalizing the palette and attire. Suit/tux, shirt, tie, shoes, accessories and rental details will be posted in the Groom Squad section.

Where do I send my photo for this page?

Email a clear, well-lit headshot to rjandjaylonwedding@gmail.com and it will be added to Meet the Wedding Party.

Who do I ask if I have questions?

Bridal party: Jam (Maid of Honor) or Mitonia (Matron of Honor). RJ's Groom Squad: Travis (Best Man).
